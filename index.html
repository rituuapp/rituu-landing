What is rituu

  1. 4 simple steps

    Arrive – Engage – Express – Integrate

    Every ritual is clear, structured and easy to follow.

  2. 2–5 minute moments

    Designed for real life, not perfect routines.

  3. Body-first experiences

    Pockets of time centered around movement, breath, gestures, touch, voice and intention.

    Not self-optimization.
    Not endless meditation.

    Just simple ways to reconnect with yourself through the body.

  4. Shared by real people

    Discover rituals created by others — or share your own.

Presence, not performance

This isn't about consuming more. And it's not about experts.

It's about creating small moments of intention — together.

Before work, instead of doomscrolling, after a stressful conversation or simply in your coffee break.

People can share rituals, discover practices from others, and build a meaningful kind of digital space.

Less performance. More presence.
