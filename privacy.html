Privacy

Effective 25 May 2026

What we collect

If you subscribe, we collect your email address. That's it. We don't track you across the web, drop cookies, or run analytics on this site.

Why

We use your email to:

  • Share updates about rituu — including when we open to early users.
  • Reach out for your thoughts. We're building rituu together with the people who care about it, and your feedback genuinely shapes what we build.
  • Occasionally invite you to fill out a short form so we can hear your input in a structured way.

Every invitation is optional. You decide what to respond to, and we'll never pass your email to anyone else.

Where it's stored

Your email is held by Tally.so, the form provider we use. Tally is GDPR-compliant and based in the EU.

Your rights

Write to connect@rituu.app any time to:

  • Remove your email from our list
  • Get a copy of what we have on file
  • Ask any privacy question

Updates

If we change this notice, we update the date at the top.
